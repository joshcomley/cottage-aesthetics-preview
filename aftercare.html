Looking after your results

Aftercare

Great results continue at home. You'll always receive personalised aftercare at your appointment — this is a general guide.

General guidance only — Purdi to confirm the exact, treatment-specific aftercare wording before launch. Your personalised aftercare is always given in clinic.

In the first 24 hours

  • Avoid touching, rubbing or applying pressure to the treated area.
  • Keep the area clean and avoid makeup where advised.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and very hot showers.
  • Skip alcohol and stay well hydrated.

Over the following days

  • Protect the skin with a broad-spectrum SPF and avoid direct sun / sunbeds.
  • Avoid facials, exfoliation or active skincare until advised it's safe.
  • Some redness, swelling or tenderness can be normal and usually settles quickly.

When to get in touch

If you have any concerns at all after your treatment, please contact Purdi straight away — you're never left to wonder. Your safety and comfort always come first.
