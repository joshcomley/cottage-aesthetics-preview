More like walking into a beautiful cottage than a busy clinic

Cottage Aesthetics is a nurse-led medical aesthetics clinic in the peaceful village of Hartlebury. Created to feel different — warm, unhurried and genuinely personal — it pairs exceptional clinical standards with a calm, welcoming space our regulars simply call “The Cottage.”

Meet your practitioner

Hello, I'm Purdi

I'm a registered nurse with over 15 years of NHS and private healthcare experience, and the founder of Cottage Aesthetics. My background in plastic surgery, dermatology and reconstructive services taught me that every face is unique — and that the best results come from understanding the individual in front of me.

I believe great care is about more than the treatment itself: trust, honesty and safety are at the heart of every appointment. My goal is simply to help you look and feel like the best version of yourself — never changing who you are, just enhancing what makes you you.
