Frequently Asked Questions

A few things clients often ask. Anything else — just get in touch.

Do I need a consultation first?
Yes — every journey begins with a free, no-obligation consultation. It's a relaxed chat so you feel completely confident in both your chosen treatment and your practitioner before any decisions are made.
Will treatments hurt?
Most clients find treatments very comfortable. Where appropriate, numbing is used, and Purdi takes her time to keep you at ease throughout. Any discomfort is discussed honestly at your consultation.
When will I see results, and how long do they last?
This varies by treatment — some are immediate, others develop over a few weeks as collagen builds. Longevity depends on the treatment and the individual. Purdi will talk you through realistic expectations for your chosen treatment.
Is there any downtime?
Downtime is usually minimal and varies by treatment. You'll be given clear, personalised aftercare so you know exactly what to expect and how to look after your skin.
Why can't I book Botox® and other prescription treatments online?
Due to UK prescribing regulations, prescription treatments can't be booked directly. Instead, you enquire and Purdi arranges a face-to-face prescriber consultation to determine whether the treatment is medically suitable for you.
How do I book?
Simply use the Book Now button anywhere on the site to book online, or get in touch by phone or email. Prescription treatments start with an enquiry.
Where are you, and is there parking?
Cottage Aesthetics is in the peaceful village of Hartlebury (DY10 4JA), tucked away from the high street — with easy, free parking right outside.
What products do you use?
Only premium, medical-grade products, chosen and tailored to your individual skin concerns. Purdi will explain exactly what's being used and why.
