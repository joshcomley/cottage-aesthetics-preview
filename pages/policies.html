Deposits

A small deposit may be required to secure your appointment. This is deducted from the total cost of your treatment on the day.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Please give at least [48 hours'] notice if you need to cancel or rearrange, so the appointment can be offered to someone else. Cancellations with less notice may result in the deposit being retained.

Late arrivals

Appointments are never rushed, so timing matters. If you arrive late, your treatment may need to be shortened to protect the following client's appointment, or rearranged where necessary.

Prescription treatments

Prescription treatments (such as anti-wrinkle injections) require a face-to-face prescriber consultation to assess suitability and cannot be booked directly online.

Your comfort & safety

If you feel unwell, or have any concerns before your appointment, please get in touch — your wellbeing always comes first, and there's never any pressure to proceed.
