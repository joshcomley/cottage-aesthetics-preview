Nurse-led care

Hello, I'm Purdi

I'm a registered nurse with over 15 years of NHS and private healthcare experience, and the founder of Cottage Aesthetics — which has proudly welcomed clients for the past 18 months.

Throughout my career I've worked within Plastic Surgery, Dermatology and specialist reconstructive services, caring for patients undergoing life-changing procedures. Working alongside highly skilled surgeons gave me an exceptional understanding of skin health, wound healing, facial anatomy, and the importance of precision, symmetry and attention to detail.

These environments taught me that every patient is unique — the very best outcomes come from understanding the individual in front of you, respecting their natural features and creating results that look balanced, refreshed and authentically them.

My philosophy

Everyone deserves high-quality care — without inflated prices

Great care extends far beyond the treatment itself. Trust, honesty, safety and communication are at the heart of every positive journey. I believe you should fully understand your options, feel confident in your practitioner, and know you're receiving care based on knowledge, experience and evidence.

Enhancing our appearance should never mean fitting into a mould where everyone leaves looking identical. My goal is simply to help you look and feel like the best version of yourself — never changing who you are, just enhancing what makes you uniquely you. That means longer appointments, honest consultations, individual treatment plans, premium products and genuinely natural-looking results.
