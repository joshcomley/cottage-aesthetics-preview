Safety & suitability

Assessed individually, every time

Suitability for dermal fillers is discussed at your free, no-obligation consultation with Purdi, a Registered Nurse with the NMC, so any questions or concerns can be talked through honestly before you decide whether to go ahead.

Looking after your results

Aftercare, made simple

You'll be given clear, personalised aftercare advice at your appointment. In general: avoid touching or applying pressure to the treated area for the first 24 hours, protect your skin with SPF, and avoid facials, exfoliation or active skincare until it's safe to reintroduce them.
